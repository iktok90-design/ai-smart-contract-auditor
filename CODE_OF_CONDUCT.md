# Code of conduct

## TL;DR

Be kind. Focus on the work, not the person. Disagreement is fine; harassment is not.

## What we expect

- Demonstrate empathy and patience, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes; learn from them.
- Prioritize what's good for the community over what's good for you personally.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.

## What we don't tolerate

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or political flame wars.
- Public or private dishonesty about security findings (real audit work demands integrity).
- Doxxing, sharing others' private information without consent.
- Other conduct a reasonable professional would deem inappropriate.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, PRs, discussions, Discord, conferences, social media when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to **a GitHub Discussion in the conduct category**. Reports are read by the project leads and treated as confidential. Possible responses range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban.

We take retaliation against reporters seriously and will treat it as a separate conduct violation.
